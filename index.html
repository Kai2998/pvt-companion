Questions, answered

Is this a dating service?

It’s primarily a companionship and social-date service. You can choose a casual, friendly or romantic experience, depending on the service offered.

Is anything sexual included?

No. No sexual activity is part of the service. It’s based on companionship, conversation and social experiences.

Who can use the service?

Only Females aged 18 and over. We verify age at registration. College students can bring their college ID for the student discount.

I’m new to the city. Is this for me?

Yes. Many of our clients have just moved for work or studies. Your companion can show you around cafés, markets and weekend spots so the city feels familiar sooner.

Can a friend know where I am?

Yes, and we encourage it. Before your meetup you get your companion’s basic details and the plan, which you can share with a friend or family member.

Do I have to be in a relationship with my companion?

No. The service is for spending agreed-upon time together. There is no expectation of a relationship.

Can I choose my companion?

We’ll share basic information about available companions, including ratings from other clients. To protect everyone’s privacy we don’t share photos. You meet in a public place first, then decide.

How is the time and price decided?

The menu is a starting point. You and your companion can customise the plan and decide how long to spend together. Extra time is ₹99 per hour, and you’ll always see the full price before you confirm.

Who can book a late night outing?

Late night outings (11:00 PM – 5:00 AM) are available once you’ve had at least one daytime meeting with us. Your companion checks in with our team every hour, and a ride home is arranged at the end or your companion can drop you.

Can I cancel if I don’t feel comfortable?

Yes. Your comfort comes first. Cancellation and refund terms are shown clearly before you pay.

Can a companion cancel?

Yes. If a companion cancels, you get a 100% refund.

Can we meet privately?

First meetings always take place in a suitable public venue. Any other arrangement must follow applicable laws, venue rules and our safety policies.

How do I activate my registration?

You’ll receive a card with your ID number. We create your profile with it, so we can keep track of bookings for your safety and ours.

How can I contact you?

By phone or Telegram. See the contact details below.

Do I need to share my photos?

No. We never ask for your photos.
